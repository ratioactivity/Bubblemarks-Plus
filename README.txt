Bubblemarks (BMP)

Bubblemarks is a cozy, all-in-one desktop hub for bookmarks, music, boredom, and a tiny digital creature who lives on your screen.

This project was originally built for my own setup, then cleaned up into a public version. It works best on specific screen types (see below), but you’re welcome to tweak it to fit your setup.

Installation & Setup

Run the setup file to install.

On first launch, Bubblemarks will automatically open on the second screen if one is detected.

It is optimized for the Zenbook Pro Duo secondary display.

Important notes about displays:

If you have a second screen that is not a Zenbook Pro Duo, Bubblemarks may still open on that screen.

You may need to open Settings and manually choose the correct display.

The layout is designed for a very specific aspect ratio, so:

Things may look off on other screens

Scrollbars may appear

Some elements may not line up perfectly

💡 Tip:
Get everything positioned how you like first, then disable scrolling. That helps a lot.

Music Player (Bottom Left)

The music player has four tabs:

Main

Shows the current track from whatever other tab you’re using.

Includes volume control.

Local Music (Bubblemarks FM)

Click Add Songs to open your Music folder.

Create a folder called:

Bubblemarks FM


Add your own audio files there.

Shuffle + loop supported.

Discs

Pulls from BubblePet disc rewards.

These are Minecraft-style music discs you unlock by leveling up your pet.

You can play them here or in the main music player.

Spotify

Connect your own Spotify account.

Control playback directly from Bubblemarks.

BubblePet 🦎

Your own little axolotl buddy who lives on your screen.

Name can be customized in Settings

Sprite pack originally purchased from:
https://pop-shop-packs.itch.io

Many animations were redesigned to work properly with BubblePet.

Pet Actions

Feed → decreases hunger

Pet → increases affection

Swim → decreases boredom

Sleep → decreases sleepiness (takes a while — realism, baby)

Rest → reduces overstimulation

Notes

Sometimes animations freeze.

If that happens, just pet or feed your pet to reset him.

You can let your pet roam freely around the screen.

Click Call Back to return him to his tank.

Achievements & Rewards

Star icon = achievements menu

Unlock new pet backgrounds

Music note icon = disc menu

Every 5 levels (and some major milestones), you earn a new music disc

Discs can be played in BubblePet or the music player
